# Getting Started with Kinkang

Kinkang makes it easy to manage your Apache Kafka clusters using Cruise Control. This guide walks you through connecting your first cluster.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you'll need:
- An existing Apache Kafka cluster (self-managed, AWS MSK, or Google Cloud)
- Your Kafka broker endpoints
- API access credentials

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Sign up at [dashboard.kinkang.cloud](https://dashboard.kinkang.cloud) and create your organization.

## Step 2: Connect Your Cluster

From the Clusters page, click **Create Cluster** and provide:

1. **Cluster name** - A friendly name for your cluster
2. **Broker endpoints** - Your Kafka broker addresses
3. **Authentication** - SSL certificates or SASL credentials

## Step 3: Deploy Kinkang Engine

Kinkang automatically deploys and configures Cruise Control for your cluster. This typically takes 2–3 minutes.

## Step 4: Start Rebalancing

Once connected, you can immediately start optimizing your cluster:

- View partition imbalances in the dashboard
- Trigger automated rebalancing with one click
- Schedule regular rebalancing jobs

## What's Next

Explore the Kinkang dashboard to discover:

- **Metrics** - Real-time cluster health monitoring
- **Autoscaling** - Automatic broker scaling based on load
- **Alerts** - Get notified when clusters need attention
